# Design and copy anti-slop

Source group 3. These are review prompts, not proof that a site was AI-generated or automatic bans. For user-facing surfaces, inspect rendered pages and the approved brand brief. Label taste judgments PREFERENCE. If no visual evidence is available, quality is UNKNOWN. Objective deception, accessibility or broken-flow findings retain their actual severity. Use [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for status and source mapping.

| ID | Source item | Review criterion and evidence |
| ----- | ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| DC-01 | harsh gradients | Inspect contrast, readability and brand fit. A gradient is not intrinsically defective; cite an actual readability problem or an owner preference. |
| DC-02 | lucide icons | Review clarity, accessible labels and consistent icon use. Do not replace a legitimate icon library merely because it is common. |
| DC-03 | em dashes | Review copy for natural, specific phrasing and agreed editorial style. Punctuation alone is neither a defect nor evidence of AI authorship. |
| DC-04 | inter/geist/space grotesk | Check licensed font assets, loading, legibility and brand decisions; preserve approved typography. Font choice alone is advisory. |
| DC-05 | colored left stripe | Check whether the stripe conveys useful hierarchy, has a non-color equivalent when meaningful, and fits the design. Decoration alone is not a blocker. |
| DC-06 | fake testimonials | Verify provenance and permission for testimonial claims. Fabricated endorsement is a substantive trust defect; propose removal or honest labeling, never invent replacements. Related: LP-13. |
| DC-07 | bento grids | Review content order, responsive behavior and information hierarchy. Approved bento layouts are allowed; identify actual usability failures. |
| DC-08 | terminal window | Verify a terminal mockup demonstrates real product behavior when presented as a demo. Decorative terminals are a preference; misleading output is a claim defect. |
| DC-09 | it's not x, it's y | Inspect generic contrast slogans and replace recommendations with specific user value supported by the product. Do not ban a sentence pattern without evaluating the claim. |
| DC-10 | checkmark bullets | Check meaningful, accurate benefit claims and accessible list structure. Repeated icons are not automatically a problem. |
| DC-11 | 3 pricing tiers | Verify tiers reflect actual entitlements, billing and customer needs. Three is not inherently wrong; do not invent pricing or force paid tiers onto free products. |
| DC-12 | no real product demo | For products sold through demonstration, inspect an honest accessible demo, screenshots or walkthrough. Do not expose private data or fabricate functionality. Mark advisory or product gap according to the acquisition flow. |
| DC-13 | soft corner radius | Inspect consistent tokens and brand fit. Rounded corners are a preference, not a release blocker. |
| DC-14 | purple and black | Check contrast and approved color system; this palette is not intrinsically unprofessional or unsafe. |
| DC-15 | no skeleton loaders | Inspect perceptible loading and layout stability on asynchronous flows. Skeletons are one solution, not mandatory when another accessible progress state works. Related: WL-12, WP-05, MP-10. |
| DC-16 | radial orbs | Review distraction, contrast, rendering cost and reduced-motion behavior if animated. Static decorative orbs alone are advisory. |
| DC-17 | dot grids | Review legibility and visual density. Do not strip approved textures without evidence of harm. |
| DC-18 | sparkle icons | Inspect whether the icon communicates an actual feature and has an accessible name when interactive. A sparkle does not itself establish a defect. |
| DC-19 | animated arrows | Check distraction, layout stability, reduced motion and clear destination. Animation must not be essential to understand the action. |
| DC-20 | no TOS | Assess terms applicability and business requirements under LP-01; no blanket legal conclusion. Related: WL-16. |
| DC-21 | no privacy policy | Assess processing and disclosure under LP-11, including tracking/server logs; existence alone does not prove suitability. Related: WL-15. |
| DC-22 | hover animations | Check keyboard/touch equivalence, focus visibility and reduced motion. Do not require hover for essential content or ban useful transitions. |
| DC-23 | neon colors | Inspect actual contrast and comfort; palette labels are not objective failures. Related: LP-08. |
| DC-24 | basic pastel colors | Check contrast and differentiation. An approved pastel palette may pass; unsupported taste judgments stay advisory. |

Record brand-preserving recommendations. Evidence of a template-like appearance is not permission to redesign the project or replace its dependencies.
