# Legal, privacy and accessibility

Source group 6. Addresses legal disclosures, user privacy rights, consumer protection, and inclusive accessibility. Audits in this group require factual context regarding jurisdiction, target user demographics, and actual data flows. Do not certify legal compliance without qualified legal counsel; flag missing disclosures as unverified or requiring legal review. Use [SKILL.md](../SKILL.md) for reporting status.

| ID | Source item | Applicability, interpretation and evidence to seek |
| ----- | --------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| LP-01 | T&C's page | Contractual terms. Inspect presence and accessibility of Terms and Conditions covering user rights, permissible use, account termination, dispute resolution, and liability limits. Related: DC-20, WL-16. |
| LP-02 | Check tracking | Data privacy. Inspect client scripts, SDKs, and network beacons for analytics, session replay, marketing pixels, and ad trackers. Verify tracking matches disclosures and consent choices. Related: WL-18. |
| LP-03 | Check local laws | Regulatory compliance. Identify governing jurisdiction of the service provider and target audience (e.g., EU GDPR/ePrivacy, California CCPA/CPRA, UK DPA). Verify data residency and cross-border transfer requirements where applicable. |
| LP-04 | Add real business details | Corporate transparency and consumer law. Inspect public footer, terms, and contact pages for legal entity name, registered office address, company registration number, and official contact email. Related: WL-19. |
| LP-05 | Only collect necessary data | Data minimization principle. Inspect database schema, signup forms, and telemetry for unnecessary PII (such as birthdate, phone number, or precise location when not strictly required by the core feature). |
| LP-06 | Keyboard friendly forms | Accessibility (WCAG 2.1.1). Inspect all interactive form controls for logical tab order, clearly visible focus indicators, keyboard operation (Enter to submit, Space to toggle, Escape to close modals), and absence of keyboard traps. |
| LP-07 | Remove unsupported claims | Consumer protection and advertising ethics. Review landing page and promotional copy for unverified performance metrics, unsubstantiated guarantees ("100% secure", "instant #1 ranking"), and exaggerated statistics. |
| LP-08 | Check colour contrast | Accessibility (WCAG 1.4.3). Measure contrast ratio between text and background. Require minimum 4.5:1 for standard body text and 3:1 for large text (18pt+ / 14pt bold) and essential graphical UI controls. Related: DC-14, DC-23, DC-24. |
| LP-09 | Alt text on images | Accessibility (WCAG 1.1.1). Inspect image elements: ensure informative images have concise text equivalents conveying their purpose; decorative images must use empty `alt=""` and `role="presentation"`. Related: WL-09, WQ-17. |
| LP-10 | Refund policy | Commercial fairness and consumer rights. For paid products, e-commerce, or subscriptions: inspect explicit refund terms and eligibility criteria, processing timeframes, cancellation rights, and dispute mechanisms. |
| LP-11 | Privacy policy page | Transparency and privacy regulation. Inspect public privacy policy for identity of data controller, categories of data collected, purpose of processing, third-party sharing, retention periods, and user data rights (access, deletion, export). Related: DC-21, WL-15. |
| LP-12 | Fix accessibility | General digital inclusion (WCAG 2.1 AA). Inspect semantic HTML structure, landmark elements (`<header>`, `<main>`, `<nav>`, `<footer>`), ARIA attributes, form label associations (`<label for="...">`), and screen reader announcement support. |
| LP-13 | Remove fake reviews | Consumer protection. Verify authenticity, verifiable identities, and real customer testimonials. Fabricated reviews, AI-generated profile photos, or inventively attributed quotes constitute unlawful deceptive practices under modern consumer laws. Related: DC-06. |
| LP-14 | Check 3rd party embeds | Privacy and performance audit. Inspect embedded widgets (YouTube iframes, Google Maps, social share buttons, chat widgets) for third-party cookie dropping, tracking scripts, and performance degradation. Consider privacy-friendly alternatives or click-to-load facades. |
| LP-15 | Check copyright on images | Intellectual property safety. Verify license terms, attribution records, and commercial use rights for all photography, illustrations, icons, and media bundled in the project. Replace unverified or stolen imagery. |
| LP-16 | Cookies policy | Privacy disclosure. Verify transparent explanation of cookie types (strictly necessary, preferences, statistics, marketing), their durations, and third-party recipients. Can be integrated into privacy policy or maintained as a dedicated document. |
| LP-17 | Form consent | Affirmative consent. Inspect marketing subscription checkboxes and terms agreements. Consent must be freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous (pre-ticked boxes are invalid in strict privacy jurisdictions like the EU). |
| LP-18 | Clear button labels | Usability and accessibility (WCAG 2.4.6). Check interactive button text for clear, descriptive action verbs ("Create Account", "Download Invoice") rather than ambiguous phrases ("Submit", "Click Here", "OK"). |
| LP-19 | Check for cookie consent | Consent management mechanism. For websites using non-essential cookies/local storage in regions requiring consent: verify that tracking scripts remain blocked until the user provides affirmative consent, with equal ease of rejection and withdrawal. Related: WL-17. |
